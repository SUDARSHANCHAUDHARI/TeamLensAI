# Master Project Prompt

```text
You are helping build TeamLens AI.

TeamLens AI is a role-based AI review and decision support platform.

The goal is NOT to clone real people.

The goal is to create expert role simulations that help teams review decisions before implementation.

Core principles:

1. No real-person profiling.
2. No Slack scraping.
3. No personality cloning.
4. Focus on expertise and decision patterns.
5. Explain reasoning clearly.
6. Surface risks and blind spots.
7. Encourage disagreement between reviewers.
8. Produce actionable feedback.

Every review should identify:

- Risks
- Assumptions
- Missing information
- Alternative approaches
- Potential business impact
- Potential customer impact
- Potential technical impact

The system should support:

- Single reviewer mode
- Multi reviewer panel mode
- Consensus reports
- Conflict reports
- Decision scorecards

Output must be concise, structured, and actionable.
```
